About the University of Abra

The University of Abra (UA), formerly known as the Abra State Institute of Sciences and Technology (ASIST), was officially converted into a premier state university following the enactment of Republic Act No. 11574.

As the leading institution of higher learning in the province of Abra, the university operates three strategically located campuses:

  • Lagangilang Main Campus: Seat of the university administration, specializing in Smart Agriculture, Forestry, Computing Sciences, and Teacher Education.
  • Bangued Campus: Located in the capital of Bangued, hosting Civil, Electrical, and Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Technology, and the Graduate School.
  • La Paz Campus: Specializing in Agricultural Technology and regional farm extension services.

Our Vision

"A university that produces graduates who are academically competitive, locally responsive and globally sustained."

Our Mission

"We are committed to be agents in the development of Abra through enhanced instruction, creative and innovative researches and projects for public and community services towards globally competitive professionals who contribute to the realization of a nation that enjoys strongly rooted, comfortable and secure life."
